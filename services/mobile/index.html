Mobile Apps

Android · iOS · Cross-platform.

Mobile projects range from a prototype you can demo to investors to a production consumer product. Pick the level that matches how much of the product has to be real: a clickable concept, a standalone app, an app synced to a live cloud database, or a product with deep device integrations and real-time syncing.

The Mobile MVP starts at ₹20,000 – ₹50,000 over 2–4 weeks. Backend-powered apps add user accounts, a cloud database and push notifications, and advanced product apps are scoped over 2–4 months. Each service page lists what is not included, so the boundary between levels is clear before you commit.
