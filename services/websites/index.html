Websites

Landing pages · Business sites · Portfolios.

Four website packages cover the range from a one-to-three page personal or local-business presence to a bespoke brand site. Each package lists what is included, what is not, and how long it typically takes, so you can compare them before you ask for a quote.

Start with the Starter Website if you need a clean, mobile-responsive presence quickly (₹5,000 – ₹7,000, 2–5 working days). Move up to Basic or Business when you need more pages, SEO fundamentals, galleries or maps, and to Premium when design quality, animation or CMS and backend integration matter. All prices are indicative INR ranges, confirmed after scope review.
