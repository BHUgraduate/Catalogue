Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-25.

This website

The catalogue does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. Fonts are served from this site, not a third party. Your browser may store one preference in local storage — whether you chose the light or dark theme. It never leaves your device.

The quote form

When you send a project request we receive what you type: your name, email address, any optional phone, business and reference details, the service and budget you chose, and your description of the project. It is sent over HTTPS to a small form service hosted on Netlify, which passes it to a private Hycyon channel on Discord.

We use it only to reply to you with a scope and quotation. We do not sell or share it. If sending fails, your email app opens with the request written out instead, and it is then handled as ordinary email.

Quotes are not contracts

Prices on this site are indicative ranges. Nothing is binding until a firm quotation follows scope review. See the terms.

Questions or deletion requests

Write to hycyon.hq@gmail.com and we will delete your request on request.
