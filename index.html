Website or web app?

A website mainly presents information. A web application lets people do active work: create accounts, save information, use dashboards and process transactions.

Why isn't every app ₹20,000?

An application with just five screens can still need complex authentication, secure database design, third-party APIs and push notifications. The number of screens alone doesn't decide the engineering effort.

FAQ

Clear answers before we begin.

Policies on pricing, infrastructure and ownership.

Pricing

Can I get a website for ₹5,000–₹7,000?

Yes, provided your requirements fit the Starter Website scope: 1 to 3 simple, mobile-friendly pages with your content, social links, and a direct WhatsApp/contact CTA. It does not include a custom backend, database, user authentication, or complex interactive state.

Is the listed price final?

No. Prices shown are indicative starting figures and expected ranges. A formal quotation is issued only after reviewing your exact scope, expected user load, design requirements, and external integrations.

Infrastructure & ownership

Does the price include domain and hosting?

No. Third-party infrastructure costs (domain registration, cloud hosting, Firebase paid tiers, Apple Developer and Play Store fees) are billed directly by their providers, so you keep 100% legal ownership of your digital property. Deployment assistance and configuration are included as outlined in each package.

Can you connect Firebase and integrate custom REST APIs?

Yes. Firebase Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions, and external REST/GraphQL APIs can be implemented cleanly with error boundaries and data caching.

Development

Can I start small and add features later?

Yes. We build modular systems. Moving from a static informational website to a dynamic web application with accounts and databases does need architectural scaffolding, so we design systems cleanly so additions do not mean throwing away previous work.

Do you build Android and iOS apps?

Yes. Depending on your budget and requirements, apps can be developed natively or with cross-platform frameworks to deploy to both Android and iOS.

AI

How are AI costs structured? (Dev vs API fees)

We strictly separate development costs from third-party AI consumption charges. Our fee covers the software engineering, prompt pipeline, vector database integration, UI, and security. Model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) charge by token volume or API calls; those usage fees are paid by you directly to the provider.

Maintenance

Do you provide ongoing maintenance and redesigns?

Yes, as optional work separate from the initial build. Monthly maintenance starts from ₹5,000–₹20,000/month and covers dependency updates, monitoring, bug fixes, and minor adjustments. UI refreshes and redesigns are available as standalone projects.
